      A practical test is the last step, not the first one. Here is how booking works with Keen On Driving, and why the timing matters.
  - type: split-full

    left_content: |
      ## Before you book

      Driving tests must only be booked **alongside agreement with your driving instructor**.

      Your driving instructor cannot be held responsible for any tests booked where:

      - Your instructor is **not available** on the date chosen
      - A test is booked for a date when your instructor **does not agree that you will be ready**

      If Daniel does not think you are ready, he will tell you what still needs work. That is better than sitting a test too early and paying for a retest.
    right_content: |
      ## Test centre and availability

      Your instructor uses **Nelson Driving Test Centre** and is generally available for tests:

      - **Monday to Friday**
      - **10am to 1:30pm inclusive**

      Exceptions can be made to both the test centre used and the time of the test, but this **must be discussed** with your instructor beforehand.
  - type: markdown

      ## Booking the test itself

      The practical test fee is paid directly to the DVSA when you book, and it is separate from the cost of lessons. You can book through the [DVSA's official service](https://www.gov.uk/book-driving-test) once you and Daniel have agreed a date.

      If you are close to test standard, the [prices page](/prices/) explains the lesson options, and the [areas covered page](/areas-covered/) explains which towns Daniel teaches in.
