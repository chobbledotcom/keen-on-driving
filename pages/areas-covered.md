      ## The test centre

      Practical tests are generally taken at **Nelson Driving Test Centre**. Daniel is available for tests between **10am and 1:30pm, Monday to Friday**.

      Exceptions can be made to the centre and the time, but they need to be discussed with Daniel before you book. The [booking your test page](/booking-your-test/) explains why that matters.

      ## The roads you will actually drive

      Rossendale is a valley of small towns and connecting roads, and lessons use all of it. Early lessons stay on quiet residential streets. As your skills settle, the town centres add traffic, pedestrians and junctions where decisions come quickly. Later lessons use the routes between the valley's towns, and the faster roads out towards the A56 and M66 when you are ready for them.

      That progression is the reason the lesson is two hours long. There is time to get somewhere quiet, work on the thing that needs work, and then drive back through traffic while it is still fresh.
