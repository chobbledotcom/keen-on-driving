      ## What the two hours are for

      A driving lesson is not just time in the car. It is time to get somewhere, practise something properly, and then use it while there is traffic around. Two hours makes that possible without the lesson ending just as you have settled in.

      Lessons use the roads around the valley. Quiet residential streets are where the basics get built: moving off, stopping, clutch control, junctions and observation. Town centre traffic comes in when those are secure. The connecting routes between Rawtenstall, Crawshawbooth, Waterfoot, Haslingden and Helmshore do the rest, because that is the driving you will actually be doing once you have passed.

      When you are ready, the practical test is generally taken at Nelson Driving Test Centre, with Daniel available between 10am and 1:30pm, Monday to Friday.
