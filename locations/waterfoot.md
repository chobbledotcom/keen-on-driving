      # Driving Lessons in Waterfoot

      Waterfoot sits in the upper half of the Rossendale Valley, and learning to drive here means learning on the roads that carry on up the valley towards Bacup and Rochdale. It is real road experience: longer runs, honest traffic, and the smaller places in between, like Lumb and Water.

      Keen On Driving is Daniel Keenan's driving school, based in the valley and covering the BB4 postcodes. Lessons in Waterfoot are two hours long, one-to-one, and always in a manual car.

      ## What a lesson around Waterfoot involves

      Early lessons can stay close to home, getting used to the car and building the basics on quieter local roads.
