      # Driving Lessons in Haslingden

      Haslingden gives you the full spread: a town centre to drive through, residential streets to learn in, and routes that connect to the A56 and the M66. For a learner, that range is worth a lot. You can start where it is calm and work up, lesson by lesson, to the faster and busier roads that take you out of the valley.

      Daniel Keenan teaches across Haslingden as part of Keen On Driving, his driving school for the Rossendale Valley. He is a fully qualified Approved Driving Instructor - a qualification only around 35% of people who train for it pass nationally. Every lesson is two hours, one-to-one and in a manual car.

      ## What lessons here cover

      Early on, the residential streets around Haslingden are where the fundamentals get built: moving off and stopping, clutch control, junctions, observation. The town centre adds pedestrians, queues and decisions that come quickly. Once those foundations are solid, lessons use the routes connecting Haslingden to the A56 and M66, where speeds are higher and the driving is more continuous.

      ## Taking your test

      When the time comes, practical tests are generally taken at Nelson Driving Test Centre, with Daniel available between 10am and 1:30pm from Monday to Friday. Never book a test without his agreement that you are ready.
